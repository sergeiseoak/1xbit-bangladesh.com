What to Check Before Sending Bitcoin

Bitcoin transfers cannot normally be reversed after confirmation. Before submitting one, check the receiving address, the displayed minimum deposit and the network information in the cashier.

Bitcoin or USDT: What's the Difference for Payments?

Bitcoin has its own blockchain and a market price that changes over time. USDT is designed to track the value of the US dollar, although it comes with its own issuer and network risks.
